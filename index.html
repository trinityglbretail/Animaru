How It Works

4 steps to a fresher home.

No scrubbing. No soaking. Just simple, clean rotation every few weeks.

1

Set Up

Fill with 2–3 inches of your favorite clumping litter. Use standalone or inside your existing box.

2

Scoop Daily

Remove clumps daily to control odor and keep bacteria at bay.

3

Refill Litter

Top up to maintain at least 2–3 inches of fresh litter throughout the week.

4

Replace & Compost

After 3–4 weeks, dispose in compost or trash. No cleaning required.
